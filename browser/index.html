    02 / INPUT & RULES

    A move you can inspect.

    Use a pointer to select a white piece and then a destination marked •. If several actions reach that square, choose the ability in the destination menu. With a keyboard, Tab through controls and press Enter or Space; the two select menus provide a shorter path. Escape or Cancel clears a selection. Confirm is the only move-submission control. There is no timer.

    Every board button names its square, color and piece. Piece letters: K king, Q queen, R rook, B bishop, N knight, P pawn. Selected pieces have a thick outline; available destinations have a •. Color alone never identifies a state.

    Float Chess rules in this preview

    Both moves resolve simultaneously. Two pieces meeting on one destination are removed (Feeding Frenzy); black has the existing engine's reserve-steal bonus. A literal swap changes both pieces' colors (Crossing the Streams). Moving into a vacated square is a Ghost Move. A queen capturing a queen removes both.

    Rooks can leave a Sewer Slide trail; pieces landing on it next turn are slowed. Pawns can use Red Balloon for a two-square push after moving. Those abilities are once per player. Bishops' orthogonal Mirror Shift and knights' extended Float Jump are tracked once per piece in the engine. Menus list only currently valid choices, including castling and promotion.

    This review follows the repository engine, with unresolved design calls for special geometry, en passant, queen interactions and multi-king checkmate. Simultaneous en passant is not offered before the opponent's hidden commitment is available to validation. No ordinary-chess engine is substituted. Inspect the rules source.

    The chapter encounter returns after three turns without a winner, or earlier if the engine finishes or you leave. Completion from the engine enables replay. You can also end a match to inspect it without a winner. A restart begins a new local session. Story progress is saved in this tab where storage is available. Reloading an unfinished encounter returns to Ink as leaving; it never invents an engine result. No participant data is stored remotely.

    Text can be enlarged. The board may scroll within its own region; coordinate menus remain available. The decorative symbol breathes slowly; Reduce Motion keeps it steady. Gameplay has no timing-dependent input. Physical Safari, VoiceOver and assistive-device review remain pending.
